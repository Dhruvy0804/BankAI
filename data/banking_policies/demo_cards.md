# BankAI fictional card policy

## Lost card and blocking

For a lost fictional card, retrieve the signed in customer's card list and identify the intended card using its masked number. If more than one card matches, ask which card. A block request only prepares a pending action. The customer must review and explicitly confirm that specific card before its status changes to blocked. Cancellation leaves the card unchanged. A chat message saying yes does not confirm the request. Never ask for a full real card number, PIN, CVV, or OTP.

## Card fees and replacement

BankAI does not publish a card replacement fee, annual card fee, refund schedule, or delivery promise. Card replacement, card unblocking, and real fraud reimbursement are not supported demo actions. Explain this limitation rather than inventing a fee or promising completion. A user may explicitly request a support ticket about a fictional card issue.
