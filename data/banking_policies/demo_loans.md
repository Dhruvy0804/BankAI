# BankAI fictional loan policy

## Demo loan eligibility

These rules apply only to fictional BankSim applications. Verified demo KYC is required. Monthly income minus monthly obligations must be positive. The proposed monthly EMI must not exceed 40% of that remaining income. The requested principal must not exceed 24 times monthly income. Equality at either limit is allowed. Use the eligibility tool with the customer's actual demo KYC and supplied financial inputs to determine the result. Meeting these rules is simulated eligibility, not credit approval.

## Loan application documents

The BankAI demo does not require uploading real identity documents, salary slips, bank statements, or a passport. It uses the existing fictional KYC record and user supplied loan type, requested amount, annual interest rate, tenure in months, monthly income, and monthly obligations. Never submit real banking credentials or identity documents. Requirements of real lenders are outside this policy.

## EMI calculation and interest rates

EMI means equated monthly instalment. The calculator uses the reducing balance formula with monthly interest equal to the supplied annual percentage divided by 1200. A zero interest loan has principal divided by the number of months as its monthly payment. Results are rounded to two decimal places. The demo has no advertised or guaranteed interest rate; the user must supply the rate, principal, and tenure. Ask for missing inputs instead of inventing them.

## Loan submission confirmation

A loan request prepares an expiring application for review. It does not submit or approve the loan. The signed in customer must explicitly confirm the exact application through the protected confirmation control. Eligibility is checked again at confirmation. A successful confirmation creates a fictional submitted application, not an approved or disbursed loan. A chat message saying yes is not confirmation.
